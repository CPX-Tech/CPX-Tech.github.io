Why CPX

The hardware, the contract, and where it runs.

The machine

  • The hardware. NVIDIA B300, eight GPUs per node, 288GB of HBM3e per GPU and 2.1TB of GPU memory per server.
  • Built to your spec. NVLink at 1.8 TB/s and 800G networking, configured to your architecture and storage profile before it goes in the rack.
  • Bare metal. No hypervisor and no shared tenancy. You get root access and run what you want.

The term

  • Yours for the term. Nodes are allocated to you for the whole term. They are not pooled or shared, and they do not get reassigned when a larger customer turns up.
  • No limits on use. The node is yours around the clock, with no preemption, no burst limit and no penalty for running it at full utilisation.
  • About five weeks. That is the time from signature to a cluster you can log into, built to your configuration.

The place

  • Vancouver, Tier III. High density power and cooling, network presence in the downtown carrier hotel, and onramps to the major clouds.
  • Hydroelectric power. About 90 percent of British Columbia's electricity is hydroelectric, so your cluster runs on a clean grid that is already there.
  • Open across North America. Canadian and United States sites, so you can put the cluster in the jurisdiction your workload needs.

Where it runs

Vancouver, Tier III, on hydroelectric power.

CPX compute runs in eStruxture's VAN-2 and VAN-3 facilities, with high density power and cooling built for AI workloads. Network presence at Cologix in downtown Vancouver puts us in the Harbour Centre carrier hotel with direct access to the Vancouver Internet Exchange and onramps to AWS, Azure and Google Cloud.

British Columbia's grid is hydroelectric, so the power behind your cluster is renewable without building a generation project for it. Latency across the Greater Vancouver region runs around 5 milliseconds. For teams with existing infrastructure in a hyperscaler region, the cloud onramps mean a hybrid architecture works without replumbing your network. United States sites follow the same standard.
