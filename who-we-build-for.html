Who we build for

Four teams who need the whole machine.

Model builders who need the node to stay put, regulated buyers who cannot share a physical host, studios whose render deadlines do not negotiate, and partners who would rather resell inference than rack it. Different problems, one answer: the whole machine, for the whole term.

AI training
and inference

AI startups and scale-ups

Teams training and serving their own models who want frontier hardware without a hyperscaler in the middle. The hard part is usually getting enough contiguous GPU time to finish a run, on terms a Series A board will sign.

Data that stays
in the country

Regulated and public sector

Banks, hospitals, universities and government, plus the software companies trying to sell into them. For those vendors, being able to name the exact facility is often what gets the account approved.

Render
and simulation

Film, VFX, animation and games

Studios with render, simulation and generative pipelines that would rather keep the work, and the footage, in the same city. Game production sits in the same building as often as not, and asset generation now runs continuously rather than at milestones. Unreleased material usually comes with contract terms about where it can be stored.

Inference
by the token

Infer Street, for partners who resell

Our inference layer, sold by the token rather than the node. Telecoms and platforms that want to put AI in front of their own customers point their existing API gateway at ours, keep their brand on the front, and price it themselves. The capacity, and the service level that comes with it, passes straight through from us.
